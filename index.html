1 How to participate

An interactive, mobile-based quiz where you scan a QR code to reveal each question — mirroring the real warehouse habit of scanning a barcode to pull up package information.

  • Scan a QR code to unlock the live question
  • Read it on your phone, then answer on the main screen
  • Two sections: UPS operations and QA practice — 10 questions

2 What you will learn

You will sharpen your picture of the UPS operation you support and of the QA fundamentals behind the work — while getting a feel for how scanning and tracking drive the whole business.

  • UPS operations, terminology and tracking
  • QA fundamentals: test types, severity vs priority, UAT
  • Quick thinking under a live clock
